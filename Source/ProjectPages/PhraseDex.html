This application was developed in a period of about 2 months for a university mobile application development module, in which I was tasked to make a useful application utilising sensors and APIs. My application received a grade of 82, being a smart concept with solutions to multiple problems.

Problem & Concept

My application solves the problem of technology not yet leveraging its potential to provide a practical advancement upon the phrasebook. Translation apps are impractical when travelling, and specific phrasebook applications prioritise the speed of searching words, rather than offering words as it predicts they are needed.

The purpose of this application is to optimise convenience.

I formed a persona and user journey to identify pain points and opportunities towards my design.

Systems Design

These are the application's features:

  • Automatically suggests phrases based on context
  • Enables saving of phrases
  • Saved phrases can be accessed offline
  • Phrases are categorised by context for quick search
  • Can translate from multiple languages (and scripts)

These are the contexts for which phrases are generated:

  • Location (e.g. airport, bar...)
  • Temperature
  • Time of day
