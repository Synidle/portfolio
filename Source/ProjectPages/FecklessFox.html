Feckless Fox was created for a university assessment requiring the creation of an interactive 3D environment in the Unity game engine. My solution was to create a 3D platforming level with a custom character controller, with a complex level design with multiple paths and areas, multiple collectables, and an upgrade item enabling further exploration on replaying the level. I received a grade of 91 for this project: "an exceptional experience through and through... like a demo of a professional game akin to the inspiration example mentioned in the report", with "complexity and fun coming from the rich level design" and being "easy pick up and play for all audience types".
