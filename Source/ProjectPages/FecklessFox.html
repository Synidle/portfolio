Feckless Fox was created for a university assessment requiring the creation of an interactive 3D environment in the Unity game engine. My solution was to create a 3D platforming level with a custom character controller, with a complex level design of multiple paths and areas, multiple collectables, and an upgrade item enabling further exploration on replaying the level. I received a grade of 91 for this project: "an exceptional experience through and through... like a demo of a professional game akin to the inspiration example mentioned in the report", with "complexity and fun coming from the rich level design" and being "easy pick up and play for all audience types".

Feckless Fox is inspired by 3D platformers of the '90s and early '00s, such as Mario 64 (Nintendo, 1996), Sonic Adventure (Sonic Team, 1998), and Rayman 2 (Ubi Pictures, 1999), aspiring to achieve the same timelessness achieved by an aesthetic appealing to children and adults alike, and accommodation of all skill levels.

Level Design

My level's design had the following objectives:

  • Teach a newly-unlocked mechanic.
  • Teach the player the level's mechanics using the environment.
  • Appropriate for an early (2nd or 3rd) level.
  • Appropriate for different skill levels.
  • Rising challenge.
  • Platforming on organic elements.
  • Build an atmosphere.

Additionally, I wanted to convey a kind of narrative through level design alone, without dialogue; and moreover have the player have particular feelings during different parts of the level, and depending on how the level is approached.

The level, taking a lot of inspiration from Bloodborne's (FromSoftware, 2015) Central Yharnam, has a dark atmosphere. This juxtaposition with the child-friendly 3D platformer genre of the game makes it more interesting and increases the scope of what can be done by the game, and also broadens the target audience. The platformers mentioned as the main inspirations similarly increase their scope by introducing unsettling environments or darker themes; Feckless Fox forefronts this more greatly.

The setting of the level is inspired by the authentic town environments of Sonic Unleashed (Sonic Team, 2008), which are fun to explore and make the world feel real and immersive.

Gameplay

While the game is best played with a controller, I ensured that it can also be played with a keyboard, to increase its accessibility.

Challenges Overcome

When having a friend test the level, he climbed up the fallen lamppost at the start — which I had not foreseen — so breaking out of bounds. I appreciated this creative use of organic decorative pieces as traversable level pieces, so rather than preventing walking up the lamppost, or completely sealing off the area it led to, I incorporated that area into the level as a secret, and hid an item there to reward attentive players.
