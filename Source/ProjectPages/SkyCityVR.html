Design

With SkyCityVR, I challenged myself to create a game entirely suited to Virtual Reality, specifically in response to my question of how to create an immersive locomotion system for VR.

I wanted a movement system that befits the advantages of the motion controllers whilst simultaneously befitting the game. This led to me formulating a design objective as follows:

To achieve a high degree of immersion by abstracting all interactions that cannot be satisfyingly reproduced in VR, then focussing on high-quality implementation of those that can, towards a fun, fluid, and tactile game.

Accessibility
