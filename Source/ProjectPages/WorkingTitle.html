Design

The problem I wanted to solve was effectively combining the emotional quality of great horror games with engaging core gameplay and combat.

Survival and psychological horror games can be effectively scary but the gameplay is often intentionally simplistic or repetitive. On the other hand, "action" horror games tend to forego the fear and helplessness felt in true horror games in favour of fast-paced combat.

I decided to try a fusion of survival and psychological horror with dungeon-crawler, because I could tap into features of dungeon-crawlers complementary to horror:

  • Journeys into the unknown.
  • Threatening encounters.
  • Secrets.

The theme of the unknown, facilitated by dungeon crawlers, pairs particularly well with psychological horror, so I decided to lean into this.

To strike a balance betwen the engaging gameplay of dungeon crawlers and the sense of vulnerability in survival horror, beyond standard enemies I decided to add a persistent threat that could not be overcome through normal means.

The design was informed by an extensive and thorough research endeavour concerning relevant games. The greatest consequence of this was a list of what to avoid with my project.
