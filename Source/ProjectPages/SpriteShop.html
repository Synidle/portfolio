This web application was developed in a period of about 2 months for an interactive web-application development module at university. My application received a grade of 86, remarked as having an engaging and rather addictive user experience.

N.B. I do not maintain this project so it does not work as smoothly nor reliably as it did at submission.

Game Design

A core feature for design was a game that could be played to increase monetary balance at the risk of losses. I had the following requirements:

  • Played by two (player & computer)
  • Balance of skill and luck
  • Easy to pick up
  • Fun and addictive gameplay
  • Unpredictable: the game can be turned around at any time

I wished this to be a card game, to suit the "casino" context, inspired by the minigames from Super Mario 64 DS (Nintendo EAD, 2004). However, no existing card games comfortably fit my requirements, so I created my own: Court Jester.

I was inspired by the fun and straightforward gameplay of Uno (Merle Robbins, 1971) and the unpredictability of Exploding Kittens (Lee, E. & Small, S., 2015) — both of which work best with sizeable groups of people, so requiring adaptation towards my primary requirement.
