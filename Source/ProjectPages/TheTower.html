The Tower is a demo developed independently as a final project in university, with conception, planning, and development taking around eight months in total. The demo is approximately two hours in playtime, with rudimentary assets, mechanics, and content, with the focus of realising the core idea behind the ambitious project within the short timeframe.

The aim of the project was to realise and sustain a sensation of sublime in a 3D action roleplaying game. The efficacy of both The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion (Bethesda Game Studios, 2006) and Dark Souls (FromSoftware, 2011) in this realm has been analysed by Martin (2011) and Vella (2015) respectively; and the foundation for this project draws upon that literature whilst critiquing and developing upon it.
